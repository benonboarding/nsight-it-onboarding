Technology Overview
1
Power on your Mac and complete OOBE
Out-of-box experience. These are the first setup screens you see when turning on a new Mac.
~3 min
  • Press the power button on your MacBook
  • Follow the on-screen prompts and select language, region, and Wi-Fi
  • When asked to sign in, use your Nsight email and temp password
  • Do not sign in with your personal Apple ID. Use your Nsight credentials only.
See screenshots of every screen (15)
2
Let iru finish setting up your Mac
iru is Nsight's device management tool. It installs automatically after you sign in.
~2 min
  • After signing in you will see iru Liftoff open automatically. This is normal.
  • Let it run until it shows all checkmarks. Do not close it.
  • Your Dock will be customized automatically with Nsight apps
  • FileVault disk encryption will enable. This protects company data.

3
Set up Google Authenticator and enable 2FA
Two-factor authentication protects your Nsight account. You need your phone for this step.
~3 min
  • Download Google Authenticator on your phone from the App Store or Google Play
  • Go to myaccount.google.com on your Mac and sign in with your Nsight email
  • Navigate to Security, then 2-Step Verification, then Get Started
  • Select Authenticator App and scan the QR code with your phone
  • Enter the 6-digit code to confirm - 2FA is now active
Do this before anything else
Setting Up Google Authenticator and 2FA

4
Set up 1Password
1Password is Nsight's password manager. You were invited via your Nsight email.
~3 min
  • Check your Nsight email for your 1Password invitation and click Accept
  • Create your account and save your Emergency Kit PDF - do not skip this
  • Install the 1Password browser extension in Chrome
  • Install the 1Password desktop app on your Mac
  • Install the 1Password app on your phone
Save your secret key

5
Set up Chrome with your Nsight account
Chrome is Nsight's default browser. Make sure you are signed in with your Nsight email, not your personal account.
~1 min
  • Open Chrome from your Dock
  • Click the profile icon in the top right and sign in with your Nsight email
  • Turn on Chrome Sync when prompted
  • Make sure Chrome shows your Nsight name in the top right, not a personal account

6
Accept your Slack invite and sign in
Slack is how the Nsight team communicates. Your invite was sent to your Nsight email.
~2 min
  • Check your Nsight email for your Slack invitation
  • Click Join Now and sign in with Google - use your Nsight account
  • Download the Slack desktop app and sign into nsightcare.slack.com
  • Install Slack on your phone and sign in as well
  • Feel free to introduce yourself in #general once you are in
Stay connected

Confirm your equipment
Make sure everything that was ordered for you has arrived before orientation day.
~1 min
  • Check that your MacBook is in hand and powers on
  • Confirm all peripherals you selected have arrived
  • If anything is missing, reply to your IT welcome email or submit a support ticket so the team can resolve it quickly
  • Reporting missing items early helps the IT team resolve issues faster
